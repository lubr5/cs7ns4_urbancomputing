How loud is it where you are?

This application compares the noise your phone hears with the noise measured by Dublin City Council's monitors around the city. Record with your phone, open the map, and you see how your surroundings compare with the nearest monitor, with your local area and with Dublin.

Why noise matters?

Noise in a city is easy to get used to and easy to dismiss, but health researchers treat it as a real risk. A Harvard Medicine magazine feature (Stephanie Dutchen, Spring 2022) summarises the evidence. It links long-term noise, especially from traffic, to:

  • hearing loss, tinnitus and sensitivity to sound
  • heart and circulation problems, and type 2 diabetes
  • disturbed sleep and stress
  • memory and attention problems, and learning delays in children

As more of the world's population lives in cities, more people are exposed. Protecting hearing and health also means fewer people needing care later.

How it works

  1. The Sensor Logger app records the microphone and your location and sends them to this site
  2. Dublin City Council's noise monitors publish a reading every 15 minutes
  3. A phone microphone measures on its own scale, so readings are converted into estimated decibels (dB(A)) using a calibration made next to one of the council's monitors.
  4. The Map shows how much louder or quieter you are than the closest monitor, your 3km radius, and the city overall. Insights shows your own history by time of day and by place.

Set up your phone

  1. Install Sensor Logger and switch on the microphone and location.
  2. Make up a token of characters
  3. In Sensor Logger's HTTP push setting, use this address, with your own token on the end: 
    /data?token=YOURTOKEN
  4. Type the same token into the box at the top of the page
  5. Start recording

Calibration

  • Calibration was done once, at Chancery Park, beside one council monitor, on a day when heavy machinery was working nearby
  • Council readings arrive 10 to 15 minutes after they are measured

Sharing on the map

  • Sharing is optional. Shared readings leave the map after 1 hour.
  • On the Share page, Clear my data removes everything you have shared
